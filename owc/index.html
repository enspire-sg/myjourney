Booth sales is one of the fastest ways to gain new leads, closings, and experience in direct sales. Those who take initiative and push beyond their comfort zones will achieve higher sales, higher commissions, and more bonuses.

Section 1: Who to Look For?

They will provide guidance, answer questions, and ensure that booth operations run smoothly.

  • Aquila Operations IC: Keson (+65 8899 4274)
  • Synergy Operations IC: Gaile (+65 8857 0902)

If you have any doubts, reach out to the respective Operations IC

Section 2: Mindset for Booth

Booth sales are a game of persistence, energy, and strategy. The right mindset makes all the difference between success and failure.

Key Mindsets to Adopt:

✅ Every passerby is a potential lead – At booths, opportunity walks past you every minute. Don’t judge too quickly — your next closing could be someone you almost ignored.

✅ Energy attracts, hesitation repels – People are drawn to enthusiasm and confidence. Smile, move with purpose, and make them want to stop and talk to you.

✅ A ‘No’ is just a step closer to a ‘Yes’ – Booth sales is a numbers game. Keep moving, keep engaging — every conversation increases your chances of closing.

Section 3: Booth Script

1️⃣ Break Ice (“Catch & Stop”)

Your only goal here is to make them stop and take the brochure. Don’t overthink. Keep energy high, smile, and make eye contact.

Body Language:

  • Stand tall, open shoulders, smile.
  • Hold brochures in a way they can see them.
  • Step forward slightly as they pass — it shows initiative.

Script Examples:

You: “Hello, do you stay around here?”
Learner: Yes.

(If they say no, this is also for those who are here in "location" These few days)

🎯 Goal: Hand out as many brochures as possible. Quantity creates opportunity.

2️⃣ Qualify (“Are they eligible?”)

Once they take the brochure and slow down or start chatting, quickly qualify them.

You: By any chance, you’re Singaporean also?
Learner: Yes.
You: Nice! We’re actually here to help Singaporeans maximise their SkillsFuture credits, whether it’s for work-related upgrading or just something you’re interested to learn as a hobby. Have you used your SkillsFuture credits before?
  • If Singaporean (eligible) → move to Program Selection.
  • If not Singaporean (not eligible) → “No worries, these are for Singaporeans — but feel free to share with friends who might benefit!” (then move on)

3️⃣ Program Selection (“Which catches your eye?”)

If they do not have any experience with SkillsFuture Courses (Common Response):

”These are some options you can learn which can be fully covered by Skillsfuture Credits. Some can use your $4000 Midcareer Credits as well. Which program would you like for me to share more info on?”

(Hand brochure / point to program board)

If they seem unsure:

“That’s okay! We can also look through a few popular program categories together. Some common choices include AI, baking, Tiktok Marketing.”
| “Would you prefer to learn AI or baking?”

If they have used their credits / have experience with SkillsFuture Courses Before:

“That’s wonderful! Which program did you go for previously?”
| “What was your experience like?”
(Let them share)
“Thank you for sharing! Anyways, which other programs here would you be keen on?”
(Show them the list)

4️⃣ Bring them back to the booth/Seniors (“Close inside”)

Once they pick a program, don’t close outside — always bring them to the booth for credibility and better conversion.

“Nice choice! Let’s head over to the booth — I’ll show you the next class dates and how it works.”
| “Perfect! I can share more details with you at the booth — come, come, it’s easier there.”

5️⃣ At the Booth – Lock In

  • Give 2–3 date options.

Creating Your Dream List using the SMARTS Principle

To turn your dreams into reality, simply writing them down isn’t enough. You need to translate your dreams into SMARTS (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-Bound, Stretched) goals.

  • Specific: To aim for a specific goal, ask yourself: What exactly do I want to accomplish? Who and what is involved? Where’s it going to happen? When you are able to describe your dreams as though you have already achieved it, it means that you are highly likely to achieve it in the long term. A specific dream isn’t “I want to be rich”; it’s “I want to have a net worth of $1 million by the age of 30 through Enspire.”
  • Measurable: Think about how you can track the progress of your dream. What will you see, hear, or feel when you achieve it? A measurable dream isn’t just “I want to lose weight.” It’s, “I want to shed 20 pounds.”
  • Achievable: Are you able to take action towards achieving your goals?
  • Relevant: Include dreams that Enspire can help you to achieve faster than the conventional route.
  • Time-Bound: Lastly, set a deadline in terms of MM-YY for your dream. When do you want to achieve it? Having a timeframe makes it urgent and pushes you to act. It’s the difference between “I want to get a car” and “I want to get a BMW I8 blue colour car by Dec 2024.”
  • Stretched: Challenge your imagination by immersing yourself in Dreams that you have not dreamed before probably because of limits that society placed on our mindset. Set dreams that you feel excited achieving and beyond what is considered conventional.
Include these below milestones in your 8/10 Dreamlist and when (MMYY) do you wish to achieve each milestone:

Start → $3k/month → $5k/month → $10k/month → $100k Savings → $300k Savings → $10k Passive Income/month
